The full browser suite passes (419.842 s), along with WebAPI, V8, CDP, network
and scheduler. One package-level targeted race run covers Headers, Fetch,
Request, Response and Worker tests and passes (67.319 s). It is not a full browser
race result. Opt-in lifecycle/profile checks and Goja native frame-microtask
support remain explicit skips in the ordinary suite; receipts list them.

representatives remain 19 matches. Every normalized observation leaf is compared

corpus remains 40 differences, brand corpus 556, and state relations two, with

The frozen fixed set's nine observational groups, 31 divergent probes and 209
